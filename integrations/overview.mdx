title: "Integrations"

---

Bounty integrations connect customer-authorized platforms to the Bounty marketing operations workspace. Customers choose which systems and accounts to connect, and Bounty uses the resulting data for reporting, analysis, and marketing-action workflows.

Connecting a platform does not give Bounty access to accounts the authorizing user cannot already access. Each connection and its selected accounts are isolated to the customer's Bounty organization.

## Google Ads

The [Google Ads integration](/integrations/google-ads) lets advertisers, agencies, and marketing teams connect their own authorized advertiser accounts. Bounty reads account, campaign, ad group, ad, keyword, and performance information for reporting, campaign analysis, and recommended marketing actions.

The Google Ads page explains the OAuth connection process, data Bounty uses, current capabilities, and security controls.

## Other Documented Connection Types

Bounty also documents support for GA4, Meta Ads, HubSpot, Snowflake, and Tinybird connections. Available data and setup steps depend on the platform and the customer's configuration.

See [Data Hierarchy](/data-hierarchy) to understand how connectors, connections, tables, and data sources work together in Bounty.
