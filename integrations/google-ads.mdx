## Overview

Bounty is a marketing operations platform used by advertisers and client teams. Customers connect their own Google Ads accounts so Bounty can analyze advertising performance, combine it with other authorized business data, and produce reporting, findings, and recommended marketing actions.

The integration is an external-user tool available to Bounty customers. A customer decides whether to connect Google Ads and which accessible advertiser accounts to include in its Bounty organization.

## Supported Capabilities

The current Google Ads integration can:

- Connect Google Ads through Google OAuth.
- Discover the Google Ads accounts available to the authorized Google user.
- Let the user select one or more active advertiser accounts.
- Read account, campaign, ad group, ad, keyword, and performance information used by Bounty reporting and analysis.
- Use that data in Bounty's campaign analysis, reporting, and marketing-action workflows.

These capabilities use Google Ads data for analysis and recommendations. Bounty does not currently create, edit, pause, or otherwise modify Google Ads campaigns through this integration.

## Connection Workflow

1. An authenticated Bounty user selects **Connect** for Google Ads.

2. Bounty redirects the user to Google OAuth.

3. Google shows the requested Google Ads permission.

4. The user authorizes access.

5. Google returns the authorization code directly to Bounty.

6. Bounty exchanges the code on the server and stores the resulting credential securely.

7. Bounty lists the advertiser accounts accessible to the Google user.

8. The user selects the accounts they want connected.

9. Bounty scopes subsequent access to the selected Bounty organization and Google Ads accounts.

Bounty never asks for the user's Google password. Customers do not provide or receive a Google Ads developer token. Bounty's developer token and OAuth client secret remain server-side, and OAuth access and refresh credentials are never returned to the browser. Stored credentials are scoped to the customer's Bounty organization.

## Data Used

Bounty reads the Google Ads business data needed for its current reporting and analysis features:

- Account identifiers and account metadata, used to identify and display the selected advertiser accounts.
- Campaigns and campaign status, used to organize and filter performance reporting.
- Ad groups, ads, and keywords, used to analyze performance at the levels where marketing teams make decisions.
- Spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, and related performance metrics, used to calculate trends, compare results, and support findings.
- Campaign configuration or bidding-strategy information where it is used by existing Bounty reporting or diagnostic features.

Bounty uses this information within the connected customer's organization for campaign analysis, reporting, and marketing-action workflows.

## Security And Access

- OAuth access and refresh credentials are encrypted at rest.
- Credentials are used only by server-side Bounty services and are not returned to the browser.
- A connected Google user can grant access only to Google Ads accounts they are already authorized to access.
- Selected advertiser accounts and credentials are isolated by Bounty organization.
- Users can disconnect Google Ads in Bounty and can also revoke Bounty through their Google Account permissions.
- Bounty does not sell credentials or expose its Google Ads developer token to customers.

For more information, see [Security Controls](/security-controls) and the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Intended Users

The Google Ads integration is available to external Bounty customers, including advertisers, agencies, and marketing teams managing their own accounts or client accounts they are authorized to access. Using the integration requires an authenticated Bounty account and access to the relevant Bounty organization.

## Product Walkthrough

1. In Bounty, open **Connections**, find Google Ads, and select **Connect**.

2. Review Google's consent screen and authorize the requested Google Ads access. The authorization happens on Google; Bounty does not receive the user's Google password.

3. After Google redirects back to Bounty, review the active advertiser accounts available to the authorized Google user.

4. Select one or more advertiser accounts to connect to the current Bounty organization.

5. Use the connected data in Bounty reporting, campaign analysis, and marketing-action workflows.

The account list is limited to accounts available to the authorizing Google user. Selecting an account for one Bounty organization does not make it available to another organization.
